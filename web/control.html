Stock reader

Pick where the game comes from, then Get the picture.
Setting it up
  1. Pick the game's picture: the capture card, or, if vMix or OBS already uses it, their virtual camera (vMix External output, OBS Virtual Camera) or a projector window.
  2. At the start of a game (full stocks), Get the picture, then drag a box tightly around the left crew's player's stock icons (the little character heads under their %), and the right crew's.
  3. Turn on Read the game. Each stock a crew loses comes off its score. A player who carries stocks into the next game and self-destructs down to them isn't counted twice.
